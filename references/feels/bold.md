# Bold

Loud, graphic, sure of itself.

Exact values: run `node scripts/feel.js bold` (intensity 1 to 3 with `--intensity`).

## Signature moves

- Quick, punchy ease-in-out (200 ms, strong curve) and a firm spring (650 / 30).
- Small radius (6 px), strong shadow (6 / 16 px at 20%).
- Heavy weight (750), tight tracking (-0.025 em), big scale jumps (1.4), full contrast.

## Do

- Use for headlines, hero CTAs, campaign moments.
- Fewer, bigger elements.

## Don't

- Low contrast or thin type.
- Lots of small motion. Bold moves once, decisively.

**Blends well with:** precise, tactile, playful.
**Pulls against:** calm, quiet, soft. Blending is allowed; the engine averages each value, so the result sits between the two.
