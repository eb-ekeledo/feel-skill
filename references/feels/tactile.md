# Tactile

Physical and pressable. You can almost feel the click.

Exact values: run `node scripts/feel.js tactile` (intensity 1 to 3 with `--intensity`).

## Signature moves

- Short, snappy transitions (160 ms) with a stiff spring (600 / 28) that lands with a tiny settle.
- Deep press (0.94): the element visibly gives under your finger.
- Tight, dark contact shadow (3 / 6 px at 18%) plus a strong top highlight (50%), like a moulded key.
- Heavier labels (600) so controls read as objects.

## Do

- Pair every press with a state change people can see: depth, shadow, or highlight.
- Use on buttons, toggles, keys, cards you drag.

## Don't

- Long, floaty transitions. Tactile means immediate.
- Flat surfaces with no light source. Without highlight and shadow there is nothing to touch.

**Blends well with:** bold, playful, precise.
**Pulls against:** calm (slow), quiet (no depth). Blending is allowed; the engine averages each value, so the result sits between the two.
